# OMNIwx First 100 Testers

Last reviewed: November 14, 2026

## Cohort Plan

Recruit in four waves so one unstable release does not create 100 simultaneous
support cases.

| Wave | Size | Focus | Exit signal |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Founder circle | 10 | Install/update, onboarding, everyday weather, crash/blank states | 8 complete core tasks; P0 defects triaged |
| Weather/storm | 25 | Radar products, source/freshness, Maps controls | No false-current display; camera/playback acceptance trend |
| Astronomy/marine/outdoor | 25 | Specialty hierarchy, missing data, planning value | Users explain the answer without coaching |
| Mixed Android beta | 40 | Device/font/network diversity, retention, value | Stable week-one use and support load |

## Acquisition

- Invite existing trusted testers personally.
- Ask astronomy clubs, storm-spotter communities, boating/angling groups and local
  outdoor groups for moderator permission before posting the tailored draft.
- Use a simple intake form or email alias; collect device model, Android version,
  interest area and permission to contact. Do not request precise location.
- Give each wave one clear testing mission and a release/build number.

## Tester Onboarding Guide

1. Install only from the approved Play testing link supplied directly by OMNIwx.
2. Record app version/build, phone model and Android version.
3. Choose a city or GPS and confirm Today/Hourly values appear.
4. Open Maps from a cold launch. Record time until the base map and selected layer
   are usable; report blank screens, missing controls, resets or stale timestamps.
5. Test one specialty relevant to you and note whether the primary answer is clear.
6. Increase font/display size if you normally use accessibility settings.
7. Use Settings > Beta Feedback. Include what you did, expected and observed;
   screenshots are welcome after hiding personal locations or account details.
8. Do not use OMNIwx as the sole source for life-safety, marine or aviation action.

Accounts and purchases are not enabled in the current foundation build. Never ask
testers to enter payment details outside Google Play or send credentials by email.

## Weekly Operations

- Monday: issue/release status and one test mission.
- Wednesday: review feedback and reproduce top issues.
- Friday: publish a short change summary and known limitations to testers.
- Track severity, version, device, reproducibility, owner and evidence. Close only
  after the fix is observed in a Play-installed build when device behavior matters.

Stop recruiting if a P0 defect, unsafe data label, runaway cost, provider-rights
issue or support backlog is unresolved. Growth is not the recovery strategy.
