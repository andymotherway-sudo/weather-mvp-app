# OMNIwx Marketing Strategy And Materials

Last reviewed: November 14, 2026

Status: draft assets only. Do not publish, send, or promise paid availability
until the launch blockers are cleared.

## Positioning

OMNIwx is weather for people who want the quick answer now and the deeper reason
when it matters. It combines everyday forecasts with radar, astronomy, marine,
climate, aviation context and weather learning in one Android experience.

Lead with "everyday weather with enthusiast-level depth." Do not market it as a
certified marine/aviation safety product, the only weather app users need, or an
owned real-time radar network while those claims are unproven.

## Initial Audiences

1. Weather enthusiasts and storm watchers who actively compare radar/products.
2. Amateur astronomers planning observing windows.
3. Boaters, anglers and coastal users wanting official marine context.
4. Outdoor planners who value alerts, timing and nearby-condition context.

Start with one cohort at a time so feedback is interpretable. Aviation users are
a valuable research cohort, but operational flight-planning claims are out of scope.

## First 1,000 Installs

- Earn the first 100 testers through direct founder contact and specialist groups.
- Turn recurring tester questions into useful weekly educational content.
- Publish short demonstrations of one real task: finding the best observing hour,
  comparing radar products, reading marine conditions, or checking climate context.
- Ask satisfied active testers for an honest Play review only after they have used
  a reliable release. Never gate features or rewards on positive reviews.
- Test small campaigns only after organic activation and week-one retention are
  understood. Cap each experiment and use one measurable call to action.

Suggested low-budget experiments:

- Two store-listing screenshot orders focused on daily utility versus Maps depth.
- Two 15-25 second videos with the same install/beta CTA and different hooks.
- One astronomy-community cohort and one storm-watcher cohort with tagged, non-
  location signup sources. Compare activation and week-one retention, not likes.
- A $50-$100 Play campaign only after a functioning listing and onboarding funnel.

## Weekly Content Rhythm

- Monday: "week ahead" forecast concept, clearly dated and sourced.
- Wednesday: 20-second feature lesson or wxLearn concept.
- Friday: astronomy/marine/outdoor planning use case.
- Weather-event days: calm, factual source walkthrough; never sensationalize risk.
- Monthly: transparent product update with what improved and what remains limited.

Primary metrics: activated installs, day-7 retained users, successful radar/feature
sessions, feedback rate, premium-interest rate, verified conversion, refund/churn,
support contacts per 100 active users and infrastructure cost per active subscriber.

## Google Play Draft Copy

### Short description

Everyday forecasts, radar and deeper weather tools in one Android app.

### Promotional description

See the quick forecast, then explore the weather behind it with radar, astronomy,
marine conditions, climatology, alerts and wxLearn.

### Long description

OMNIwx brings everyday weather and enthusiast-level detail together.

Start with current conditions, hourly and daily forecasts, official weather alerts,
and standard radar. When you want more context, explore astronomy and night-sky
conditions, marine weather, climatology and records, aviation weather context,
space weather, maps and educational wxLearn topics.

Built for two ways of checking weather:

- Simple views make everyday conditions fast to scan.
- wxLab opens deeper measurements and technical context when you want it.

Highlights:

- Current, hourly and daily weather
- Official weather alerts and source-aware conditions
- Radar, satellite and weather map layers
- Astronomy and space-weather context
- Marine forecasts, tides and buoy observations where available
- Almanac, climate normals and records
- Saved places, widgets and Android Auto surfaces
- wxLearn explanations for weather concepts and data

OMNIwx is not a substitute for official life-safety, marine or aviation decision
systems. Data availability and coverage vary by source and location.

OMNIwx Plus is planned, not active in the current beta. Proposed Plus capabilities
remain subject to testing and provider approval.

- "Climate context turns today's number into something you can understand."

## Short-Form Video Ideas

- "Can I observe tonight?" Open Space, reveal the useful window, then the reasons.
- "Radar is not one product." Compare broad mosaic and local radar without hype.
- "Why 20% rain does not mean rain for 20% of the day." Use wxLearn visuals.
- "Three things to check before going on the water." Wind, official forecast, tide.
- "Is today actually unusual?" Current temperature into Almanac context.
- "Simple versus wxLab." Same location, quick view then deeper measurements.

Every video should show a currently working build and real source timestamps. Do
not use simulated premium screens or claim live owned imagery that is unavailable.

## Feedback Questionnaire

1. What did you open OMNIwx to accomplish today?
2. Could you complete that task without instructions?
3. What felt slow, confusing or untrustworthy?
4. Which screen or feature would make you return next week?
5. Which feature did you expect but could not find?
6. Did a data timestamp/source/fallback ever feel unclear?
7. Which proposed Plus capability would be worth paying for, if any?
8. At $5.99/month or $49.99/year, what would Plus need to do reliably?
9. What Android model, Android version and display/font size did you use?
10. May we contact you about a follow-up test? Keep the response separate from
    usage analytics and do not request precise home/work locations.
